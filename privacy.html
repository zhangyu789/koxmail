Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and manage your information

1. Information we collect

When you register and use KoxMail, we collect only what is needed to provide the service: account identifiers, sign-in logs, device info, and interaction records. We do not collect unrelated sensitive data. Processing follows the principle of minimum necessity.

2. How we use information

We use data for account security, anti-spam, service improvement, and support — not for unrelated purposes without authorization. Processing follows strict internal standards aligned with ISO 27001.

3. Information sharing

We do not sell personal information. We disclose data only when required by law or with your consent, and only the minimum needed to service providers bound by confidentiality.

4. Data security

KoxMail uses end-to-end encryption, tiered access control, and audit mechanisms. Data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified facilities with regular backups and security reviews.

5. Compliance

KoxMail is certified under ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR, providing strong privacy and compliance for users worldwide.

6. Contact us

Questions or data subject requests? Contact us via the support center. Our privacy team responds within 48 hours.
